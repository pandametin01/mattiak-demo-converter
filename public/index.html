What is recreated?

Recorded starting positions and sampled opponent movement. Practice uses a standard weapon and health baseline. Unsupported situations are excluded, and geometry is checked in-game. The timer is a practice target, not the opponent’s measured reaction time.

    A real match is the starting point.
    We keep only situations the trainer can reproduce.
